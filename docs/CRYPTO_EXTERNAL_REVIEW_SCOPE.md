# Third-Party Independent Cryptographic Review Scope

> **Document Status**: External Audit Qualifications & Deliverables Definition

---

## 1. Auditor Required Qualifications

- Minimum 5 years specialized experience in secure messaging protocols (Signal, MLS, Noise Framework).
- Demonstrated experience auditing mobile cryptographic implementations (Android KeyStore, iOS Secure Enclave, Flutter FFI).
- Proven track record of auditing protocol state machines, Double Ratchet implementations, and key lifecycle management.
- Zero commercial or personal conflict of interest with GuffSuff project maintainers.

---

## 2. Mandatory Audit Deliverables

1. Formal Architecture & Protocol Specification Review.
2. Threat Model & Security Boundary Audit.
3. Cryptographic State Machine & Key Lifecycle Review.
4. Mobile Storage & FFI Binding Audit.
5. Multi-Device & Group Rekeying Audit.
6. Categorized Findings Report with Severity Classification (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
7. Remediation Verification & Final Residual Risk Statement.
